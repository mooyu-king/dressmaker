Why is the Finish Dress button missing?

First check which screen you are on. A dress waiting for assembly needs a different fix from a dress that has already received a customer verdict.

Check the pieces

Compare your sketch with the pieces on the mannequin. Look for a cut piece still waiting to be attached.

Start with the main garment and then inspect each sleeve and smaller part in turn. Keep the current design while checking its assembly.

Inspect the joins

Once the pieces are in place, examine each join for an unfinished seam.

Follow one join at a time. After closing a remaining seam, look for a change in the finishing action before making any other change. This comparison helps isolate the cause.
