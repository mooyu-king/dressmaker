Restart a Dressmaker commission: what do you lose?

Do not budget for a refund. A Steam player who restarted a dress after choosing the wrong material lost their garment and still needed money for the replacement. We have no equivalent browser-build test yet.

Where are you in the order?

Select your stage before deciding whether to start over.

Before cutting

If the wrong fabric is still on the bolt, read the material name in the request again. Compare it with the shop label and your available balance. A wrong purchase and a ruined garment are different problems. There is no evidence here that restarting the dress reverses the purchase.

After cutting or sewing

First identify the affected panel. Is just one sleeve wrong, or did you use the same fabric for the entire outfit? Inspect the available piece-editing options before confirming a whole-dress restart. The reported loss happened late in construction. No recovery of an individual cut piece was documented.

After delivery

Check whether the customer has already given a verdict. Redoing an active design and replaying a completed commission are separate actions. The linked replay discussion does not establish a way to roll back every submitted order. Restarting the browser cannot select an earlier story milestone.

Coins, cloth, panels and progress

In the reported Vivian commission, the player discovered after taking a photo that they had used silk instead of velvet. The table separates their observations from the outcomes still worth checking.

Coins

The player expected the silk purchase to be refunded. They reported ending with 24 coins and no replacement dress.

Before you restart: The starting balance and exact loss were not recorded. Budget for the replacement before acting.

Uncut fabric

The player still had fabric after restarting. Its material did not suit the velvet request.

Before you restart: There is no before-and-after inventory count. This does not establish that every bolt survives or that cut cloth returns to stock.

Cut panels

The assembled dress was gone. The player did not list the individual cut panels afterwards.

Before you restart: Panel recovery is unknown. Do not count on getting the same shapes back as loose pieces.

Commission progress

The work put into the current dress was lost before delivery.

Before you restart: The report does not show that the customer, the whole story or a previously submitted commission was reset.

Check the brief before buying again

Read the requirement literally. If the customer names a material, a similar colour alone cannot tell you that you have the right bolt. Write down the balance and the price of the fabric you still need. If the only problem is a missing finish button, inspect the garment first: a construction issue calls for a different check.

Will restarting the browser restore the dress?

The restart control on our player reloads the embedded game. It does not send a refund command, undo a purchase or request a previous save. An in-game restart acts inside the current edition. Keep your active session open if you only want to read advice.

Restart FAQ

Can I earn money back with the leftover fabric?

A community reply suggests making an off-the-rack dress from remaining stock and selling it. That would be a new sale, not a refund. Check that the option exists in your edition and that you can afford any other materials it needs.

Can I replay an order I already handed in?

The Vivian case happened before delivery. Players also ask about completed orders; the two situations need separate evidence. Read the submitted-commission discussion.

How can I compare the result in my version?

Use a disposable test session. Record your edition, coin balance, uncut lengths, loose panels and active request. Change just one thing. Take the same inventory afterwards, including what is still attached to the mannequin. Comparing those records avoids treating the missing dress as proof that every resource was lost.
