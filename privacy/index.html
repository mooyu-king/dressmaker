Privacy Policy

Privacy and embedded content

Visiting these pages

The site loads a traffic analytics script from data.1back.link and uses its api/send endpoint. Visiting a page sends network requests that can expose your IP address, browser information and requested URL to the services involved.

Starting the game

Selecting Start connects your browser to static.kingdomofmarionettes.org. Its game page can contact additional providers including app.pageview.app and can use browser storage under its own origin. Game and save behaviour are controlled by that provider.

Optional videos

Guide pages embed the YouTube privacy-enhanced player. Your browser may contact YouTube when the video frame approaches the viewport, before you play it. Opening the external watch link visits YouTube directly.

Your choices

You can read every guide without starting the game. Browser settings let you manage site data and block third-party resources. Following the Steam link takes you to Steam under its own policies.

Email

If you email us we receive your address and the information you send to handle your request. Contact mooyuking@gmail.com with privacy or deletion questions.
